## 👋 A Little About Me

I’m **Lavanya**, a third-year Computer Science Engineering student who enjoys building things with code and understanding what makes them work behind the scenes.

Currently, I’m focusing on **full-stack development** and **data structures & algorithms**. I learn best by building projects, running into bugs, and figuring out how to solve them one step at a time.

I’m also contributing to **GSSoC 2026**, where I get to explore real-world codebases, collaborate with other developers, and learn a lot beyond tutorials and classroom projects.

> Debugging has taught me that most problems are solvable — sometimes they just need a little patience (and a few console logs). 😄
